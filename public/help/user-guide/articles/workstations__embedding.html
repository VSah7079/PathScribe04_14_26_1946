Embedding Station

Overview

Purpose. The Embedding Station is the bench screen for the person embedding tissue in wax. You scan a cassette, check that the number of pieces in it matches the number counted at grossing, record the mold size and how the tissue is oriented, and mark the block as embedded. If something is wrong with the cassette (broken, empty, unprocessed, extra tissue) you report it with one touch. You can also reprint a cassette label that has become damaged or covered in wax, and you can link cassettes that hold one specimen's tissue split across several blocks.

Intended users. Histotechnologists and pathology assistants at the embedding bench, and laboratory managers. The screen opens only for roles that have the Embedding screen switched on; by default these are the Histotechnologist and Pathology Assistant roles.

Where it fits. Embedding follows grossing and tissue processing and comes before cutting. The block you confirm here is the block you cut at the Microtome Workstation. The piece count you compare against was recorded in Grossing. A mismatch or a reported discrepancy raises a Tissue Discrepancy item in Quality for the supervisor. The embedding step is also part of the chain of custody tracked in Batch Management. Open the screen from the Pathology Workspace (the Embedding Workspace tile) or with the Go to Bench button when your station is an embedding station.

Screen Tour

The page is titled Embedding Station. The Back button at the top left returns to the Worklist. Before a scan the screen shows only "Scan a cassette barcode to begin" and a scan box. After a scan a grey identity bar appears, with three columns below it.

Block and matrix block cassettes

This station takes block cassettes, including a cassette shared by several specimens (a matrix block, for example M1). A matrix block is embedded like any other block: piece count, mold, orientation, alerts, comments, discrepancy and reprint all work the same, and the identity bar reads "Matrix block of specimens" followed by the specimens that share it. A Tissue Discrepancy on a matrix block is raised against the first specimen in it, with the block named in the item. A cell-block cassette, a decant or a bare specimen barcode is refused with "The Embedding station takes block and matrix block cassettes. Cell-block cassettes, decants and specimen barcodes aren’t supported yet." A slide barcode opens the block it belongs to.

Carriers at this station

A card at the top of the screen, Carriers at this station, links this bench to Batch Management. A carrier is the basket, rack or tray that moves a group of cassettes between steps. Using one is optional: an item scanned on its own is still recorded at this station.

  • The list. The card lists the open carriers for this station: the ones routed to it (marked Routed here) and the ones at Embedding. STAT carriers come first, then the oldest; with none it reads "No open carriers for this station." Select one, or scan its barcode (it starts with CONT- or RACK-), to open it here.
  • A carrier from the step before. A processor basket from Processing moves to Embedding when you scan it here. It stays the same batch, and every cassette in it is recorded as received from the carrier.
  • Unloading. With a carrier open, scan each item as you take it out. The first scan starts the unloading. The card counts what has been scanned off (the line "… of … scanned off", for example 3 of 12) and lists what is still to scan. When everything is scanned off, Finish unloading closes the carrier.
  • An item that is not in the carrier. The item does not open straight away. The card says it is not in the carrier: Continue anyway opens it and records it as unexpected (this is audited), Cancel does nothing.
  • A carrier for another step. The card warns you which step the carrier belongs to. Open here anyway opens it and records that it was opened at another step.
  • A closed or unknown carrier. The card says so plainly, with the date it was completed or aborted.
  • No station set. The card asks you to choose this computer's station from the list and select Use this station. Until you do, nothing scanned here is recorded and carriers can't be opened here.
  • Seal manifest stops anything being added to or removed from the carrier.

Every scan here is recorded with this station, the time and your name, which is what the scan trail and the turnaround reports are built from. Confirm also records the block as completed at this station.

Scan history

With a case open, Show scan history (just below the carrier card) opens the case's scan trail: every station scan of its cassettes, blocks and slides, newest first. The columns are When, Item, Where (the station and the step), Who and What happened (Received, Loaded into carrier, Taken out of carrier, Matched, Missing, Unexpected, Label printed, Completed here), with the outcome when it was not OK (Wrong item, Not found, Mismatch), the carrier the item was in ("In carrier" and its barcode), and "Recorded when the carrier was scanned" for a scan made for every item in a carrier. Hide scan history closes it. While it reads the trail it says "Loading scan history…". It is closed until you open it, and it is shown only to people allowed to see scan history; if the service refuses, it says "You do not have permission to see the scan history." With nothing recorded yet it reads "No station scans recorded for this case yet."

Exhausted blocks

Batch 731. When the block at the bench is exhausted, an amber line above the work says so and what remains: "Block A3 is exhausted. What remains: unstained slides L4 and L5; other blocks of this part: A1, A2." ("none" when there are none). When orders wait for the block to be re-embedded it adds "1 order waits for this block to be re-embedded."

When the pathologist has asked for the block's remnant or wet tissue to be re-embedded, re-embed it, then select Re-embedded: release the orders on that line. The block goes back to Embedded, the orders waiting for it go to the Microtome, and the line says "Re-embedding recorded. 1 order was released to the Microtome." The button is grey without the permission to mark a block re-embedded (the roles with this station have it by default); if the save is refused it says "You don’t have permission to record re-embedding. Ask your administrator." or "Re-embedding could not be recorded. Nothing was changed." A matrix block cannot be re-embedded this way.

Batch 732: for a megablock, a multi-piece or a matrix block, a panel shows the type, the slide size it produces ("Slides: Large-format slide") and each piece with its part, description, orientation and ink, and "Used up" for a piece already used.

Hotkeys

Both keys work while this screen is open and do exactly what the button does, including the "Still required" checks. An administrator can change the keys.

Key Spoken phrase Action
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+C "confirm count" or "confirm embedding" Presses Confirm with the count in the box.
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A "add comment" or "save comment" Saves the text in the comment window, while it is open.
Foot pedal 1 none Confirms the piece count as the grossing count. Does nothing if no grossing count exists or the block is already Embedded. Set up per workstation by an administrator.
F1 none Opens this help article.

Phrases are also available in French, German, Dutch, Korean and Spanish. See Voice and Keyboard.

Using a tablet at the bench

Since Batch 735 the station screens work with a finger, including a gloved one.

  • Touch layout. On a tablet, buttons, chips and tick boxes are at least 44 px, main actions such as Confirm or Print are larger, and text in fields is 16 px. On a desktop the screen keeps its compact layout. Change this per computer with Touch layout on this computer in the station menu at the top (Auto, Always on, Always off).
  • Scanning. The scan field accepts a barcode scanner without opening the on-screen keyboard and stays ready for the next scan. To type an ID by hand, select ⌨ Type; ⌨ Scan hides the keyboard again.
  • Keyboard up. When you type in a field, the field stays in view. If the form's Save button would be hidden by the keyboard, a copy of it appears just above the keyboard.
  • Tooltips. Tap a label that has extra information to read it.
  • Next cassette. The scan field stays beside the case details while you work, so scanning the next cassette opens it.

Step-by-Step Procedures

Standard workflow

  1. Check the station indicator in the top bar names your embedding bench. If it does not, select it and choose your station.
  2. Open the Embedding Workspace tile in the Pathology Workspace. The scan box is ready.
  3. Scan the cassette barcode. The identity bar and the three columns appear. The block status shows in the Block Summary.
  4. Compare the identity bar with the cassette in your hand. Look at the alert badges and any block comments.
  5. Count the pieces of tissue in the cassette. Type the number in Observed count, replacing the number that was filled in. Do not accept the filled-in number unless you have counted.
  6. Select a Base Mold Size button. The chosen size is highlighted.
  7. Type orientation notes, or select a quick fill such as Cross-section up. The text is saved when you leave the box.
  8. Select Confirm. The status changes to Embedded, the box is locked, and "Observed at embedding" shows your count.
  9. Scan the next cassette. The new cassette replaces the one on screen.

A count that differs from grossing

If the count you confirm is different from the grossing count, PathScribe raises a Tissue Discrepancy item for your supervisor and says so on the screen. Check your count before you confirm.

Alternative workflow

  1. Confirm by foot pedal. With the grossing count known and the block not yet embedded, tap foot pedal 1. This confirms the grossing count as the observed count. Use it only when you have counted and the count matches.
  2. Confirm by voice or key. Say "confirm count" or press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+C once the box holds your count.
  3. Split cassettes. When one specimen's tissue was split over several cassettes (A1, A2, A3), scan one of them, tick the others in Multi-Cassette / Split-Block and select Group as split cassettes. The banner then lists the group and tells you whether every cassette is embedded yet.
  4. Comment on a block. Select Block comments, type in the Comment box and select Add Comment. Comments stay on the block for the next person.

Exception handling

  1. Something is wrong with the cassette. Select the matching discrepancy button: Damaged Cassette/Broken Hinge, Extra Tissue Found, Missing Tissue/Empty Cassette or Unopened Cassette/Unprocessed Tissue. The block's status does not change, "Last flagged: …" appears, Confirm goes grey, and a Tissue Discrepancy item is raised in Quality for the supervisor; the screen tells you it was raised, or that it could not be. There is no confirmation. When the problem has been dealt with, select Clear flag next to "Last flagged": one click, and Confirm works again. Clearing is recorded in the audit log with your name.
  2. The count really differs. Type the number you counted and select Confirm. The block is embedded with your count and a Tissue Discrepancy item records both numbers.
  3. The label is damaged or covered in wax. Select Reprint Cassette. In the window titled "Reason required" choose Faded barcode, Mechanical jam, Wax buildup or Other, then select Reprint. The label prints and the panel shows "Reprinted 1×, last reason: …". Cancel closes the window without printing.
  4. The cassette is the wrong kind. This screen cannot embed a cell block or decant. Ask your supervisor how your laboratory records them. (Matrix blocks are embedded here like any other block.)

A reported discrepancy stops Confirm

Reporting a discrepancy records the reason, alerts quality and switches Confirm off (also for the key, voice and foot pedal) until the flag is cleared. Clear flag does not close the Tissue Discrepancy item in Quality; the supervisor does that there.

Error recovery

  1. If "That scan didn’t match a case or block. Check the barcode and try again." appears, scan again, or type the barcode and select Go.
  2. If "Block A2 wasn’t found." appears, the block is not on that case. Check the cassette label against the case.
  3. If Confirm is grey, read the "Still required" line and fill the field it names.
  4. If Reprint Cassette reports "Reprint failed — see the message above.", the reason has already been recorded on the block, and the printer's own message is shown under the button. Fix the printer and reprint; the count will rise by one more.
  5. If Device Status says "This station isn't configured for real cassette-label printing." or "No scan station selected for this device — set one from the NavBar station indicator.", choose your station from the indicator, or tell your administrator.
  6. If a red line says "Not saved: …", the change you just made was not stored. The screen has reloaded the block as it is now (PathScribe first tried again on the newest copy of the case). Look at the block and repeat the change. Nothing was printed or raised for an unsaved change.
  7. If you confirmed the wrong count, tell your supervisor with the case number. The count box is locked once the block is Embedded.

Tips and Best Practices

Common mistakes

  • Pressing Confirm with the count that was filled in, without counting. The box starts with the grossing count, which makes a mismatch invisible.
  • Using foot pedal 1 without counting. It confirms the grossing count, so it can never record a mismatch.
  • Expecting Clear flag to close the item in Quality. It only lifts the hold on this screen.
  • Choosing a quick fill and then typing more text before leaving the box, expecting the quick fill to stay. The quick fill replaces the whole text.
  • Grouping cassettes by mistake. There is no way to ungroup from this screen; tell your supervisor.

Efficiency recommendations

  • Scan one cassette after another; a scan opens the next cassette at once, and a handheld scanner works whether or not the cursor is in the scan box.
  • Use the mold chips and quick fills rather than typing.
  • Use the keys or voice for Confirm when your hands are in the wax.

Workflow recommendations

  • Start the shift by checking the station indicator and the printer in Device Status.
  • Report discrepancies as soon as you see them, before you embed, so the supervisor can decide.
  • Use block comments to pass on anything that matters at the microtome, such as "orient edge toward blade".

Related Features

Upstream workflow

  • Grossing: the piece count you check against, and the block's tissue description.
  • Pathology Workspace: the Embedding Workspace tile.
  • Batch Management: processing steps that bring the cassettes to the embedding bench.

Downstream workflow

  • Microtome Workstation: the embedded block is cut and its slide labels printed.
  • Quality: where a Tissue Discrepancy item raised here is followed up.
  • Slide Distribution Station: finished slides.

Troubleshooting

Common user issues

Problem Likely cause
No Embedding Workspace tile Your role does not include the Embedding screen.
"Takes block and matrix block cassettes" You scanned a cell block, decant or specimen barcode.
Confirm is grey A required field is empty, a discrepancy is flagged (select Clear flag once dealt with), or the block is already Embedded.
The count box cannot be edited The block is already Embedded.
Foot pedal 1 does nothing There is no grossing count, the block is already Embedded, a discrepancy is flagged, or a required field (for example the mold size, if your organisation requires it) is empty.
Mold or orientation not kept The orientation text is saved when you leave the box; click elsewhere before scanning the next cassette.
"Reprint failed" The printer refused the label. The attempt is still counted.
A red "Not saved" line The change was not stored; the block was reloaded. Repeat the change.

Resolution steps

  1. Read the line under the button or the red message under the scan box.
  2. Scan the cassette again, or type its barcode and select Go.
  3. Check the station indicator and Device Status.
  4. If a "Not saved" line shows, the block is already reloaded; repeat the change. Otherwise reload the page and scan the cassette again to see what was saved.
  5. If a count or a mold size looks wrong after reloading, repeat it.

When to contact support

Contact your laboratory's PathScribe support when a cassette will not open although its barcode is correct, a Confirm does not stick after reloading, or a cassette label prints for the wrong block. Have the case number, the cassette label and the time ready.
